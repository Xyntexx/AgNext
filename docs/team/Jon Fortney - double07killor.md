## Bio
I come from a line of builders and problem-solvers. My dad was developing and engineering long before I was born — building narrow 20" planters and corn heads, designing a self-propelled sprayer from scratch before they were common, and even writing his own accounting and farm management software. I grew up helping him wire radar and GPS rate controllers, then later add mapping and monitoring systems. Nothing we ran came factory-equipped — if it has precision-ag features, we built or retrofitted them ourselves.

That environment hardwired a curiosity for how things work. I've always had a passion for technology, and my dad did his best to steer that passion away from video games and toward productive tinkering — and somehow supported both. By high school, I was teaching robotics and programming to younger students, leading student taught classes that started with MIT's Scratch and Spheros, progressing to C++ and Arduino-powered projects, with motion provided by Vex Robotics kits. 

Those early experiences blended my love for agriculture, engineering, and technology — shaping how I approach every project now: practical first, polished second, and built to last.

## Farming Background
I was born and raised on our family farm in southwest Wisconsin, where I still live and work today. We grow corn and soybeans, running older equipment that we’ve upgraded ourselves with GPS, rate control, and section systems.

Over the years, I’ve done everything from wiring monitors in the cab to building new harnesses and controllers from scratch. Our farm has served as both a business and an engineering sandbox — where ideas get proven, broken, and improved under real dirt conditions. That hands-on connection to agriculture keeps me grounded in why AgOpenNext matters: it’s not just software, it’s the bridge between technology and the people who rely on it to feed everyone else.

## Development Background

Current projects include **Chimera**, a precision rifle companion system combining an ESP32-S3–based hardware platform (built with ESP-IDF) and a connected **React PWA** for configuration and data visualization, and **The Shooters Hub**, a **Next.js–based social and match management platform** for competitive shooters. Both projects tie together my passion for engineering, interface design, and efficient data handling.

## Engineering Background
Engineering, for me, has always been about solving real problems with whatever tools are available. A lot of that started on the farm — designing, adapting, and repairing equipment that didn’t come with the features we needed. That mindset naturally evolved into a passion for **systems design** and **rapid prototyping**.

I’ve spent nearly a decade working with **3D printers, laser cutters, and CNC routers**, using them across ag projects, research, and even professional motorcycle racing. In both education and industry, I’ve specialized in turning concepts into working hardware efficiently — but never recklessly.

I believe in **proper planning above all else**. Every project starts with detailed documentation, clear requirements, and a solid plan before a single part is printed or machined. When it’s time to execute — whether that’s next weekend’s race, seeding season, or a system launch — things need to just work.
That’s where discipline pays off: plan carefully, build deliberately, and make it right the first time.
